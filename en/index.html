Frequently asked questions

For help with your shortlist, contact us on Telegram at @wheretomove_bot.

Do I need Telegram?+

The free questionnaire and shortlist work in your browser without Telegram. Your answers and results stay available in the same browser and aren’t automatically transferred to Telegram. Matching as a couple works through the Russian-language bot.

What do I get for free?+

A shortlist of matching cities, with their pros and trade-offs, climate, a map, and budget estimates. For each city, see what’s nearby, including selected sports and cuisines, cafés, parks, shops, transport, and healthcare. You can compare places and change your answers. Matching as a couple is available through the Russian-language Telegram bot.

When will PRO open?+

A launch date hasn’t been announced. PRO will offer visa pathways and neighborhood analysis once the data is verified. You can sign up for a free one-time launch notification in the Telegram bot.

Can I change my answers?+

Yes. Adjust your budget, climate preferences, or other criteria and see how your shortlist changes.

How does matching as a couple work?+

One person sends an invitation and their partner fills out a separate questionnaire. The shared shortlist shows how well each city suits both people.

What if no city matches?+

The report shows which requirement ruled out the remaining options and suggests an adjustment. It only changes your answers if you choose to apply it.

How do I get started?+

Select “Take the questionnaire” and answer in your browser. You can close the page and finish later in the same browser. The Telegram bot is available in Russian.
