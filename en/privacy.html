WhereToMove Privacy Policy

Version dated 19 November 2026.

Who handles your data and how to get in touch

WhereToMove is operated by Darya Dmitrievna Smirnova, an individual registered as self-employed under Russia’s professional income tax regime. To ask about data processing or to correct, obtain a copy of, or delete your data, contact @wheretomove_bot.

What data we use

  • Your Telegram user and chat IDs and the name provided by the Telegram Mini App.
  • For the browser questionnaire, a random ID in a secure cookie. The browser profile does not use a Telegram ID.
  • Questionnaire answers, their latest versions, and change history.
  • The recommendations and reports we generate.
  • The link between profiles and your shared results if you use matching as a couple.
  • Feedback you submit as free text.
  • When you request a one-time PRO launch notification, your Telegram ID and the date you signed up. You can cancel in the PRO section.
  • Technical usage events and errors. These do not contain your answers.
  • Paid access status, transaction ID, amount, currency, and refunds. We do not store card data or other payment credentials.

Why we use your data

We use data to save your progress, calculate and show personalized recommendations, sync the bot and Mini App, show shared results for couples, prevent abuse, and improve the questionnaire and its recommendations. The browser questionnaire saves your answers in this browser and on the server under a random ID. Its answers and results are not transferred to Telegram automatically. We do not sell your answers or use them for advertising profiles. We only use your PRO notification signup to send that one message.

Who receives your data

Telegram passes us Mini App data and delivers messages. Our hosting provider stores the files the service needs to run. We create encrypted backups to protect against loss. They may be stored on the operator’s secured device for up to 30 days and, once cloud backups are enabled, in Cloudflare R2 for up to three months. Cloudflare receives only an encrypted backup. Questionnaire answers are not used for other purposes. To find and check photos, Wikipedia, Wikidata, and Wikimedia Commons receive public city names and coordinates, article IDs, and image IDs. These photo services do not receive Telegram IDs or questionnaire answers. Access is limited to the operator and the technical providers that need it to perform their roles.

How long we keep data

  • Drafts and saved answers are kept for 90 days after your last activity.
  • Your most recent personal report is kept for 30 days. A report shared by private link is kept for 14 days.
  • Answer history and product analytics are kept for 90 days.
  • The link between profiles for couples is kept for 90 days.
  • Free-text feedback is kept for up to 180 days.
  • PRO launch notification signups are kept until the message is sent or the signup is canceled.
  • Your reminder preference and a minimal record of an already attempted reminder are kept until your data is deleted. This prevents restarts and repeat visits from triggering new reminders.
  • Paid access status and required records are kept as long as needed to meet our obligations and applicable legal requirements.

When the retention period ends, we delete or anonymize the data. Infrastructure backups may take longer to expire under their normal rotation schedule.

Your choices and rights

Before you start the questionnaire, the website and Mini App ask you to accept this policy and consent to data processing. Once you consent, the website sets a secure session cookie for up to 90 days. The cookie saves your progress and limits access to your own results. Local answers are stored in this browser. You can withdraw consent by deleting your data. Contact the operator to request access or a copy, correct answers, delete data, or object to optional analytics.

The “Delete my data” button in the browser questionnaire or Mini App immediately deletes the data for that profile. For Telegram profiles, the bot’s cache is cleared within one hour. In the Telegram chat, you can also enter /delete_me and then /delete_me confirm. For the website, deletion applies to the profile created in this browser. Once its cookie is cleared, another browser cannot be linked to that profile.

Data is removed from the live server immediately. The server keeps a minimal deletion record without questionnaire answers so an older calculation cannot recreate the profile after it finishes. When restoring an earlier snapshot, we first apply the deletion records from the latest available copy and then verify the data and access to reports. Deleted answers may remain in encrypted backups until they rotate out. The restore process keeps them out of the live profile.

Security and policy changes

The Mini App verifies identity using the Telegram signature in initData. The browser questionnaire uses a separate signed cookie. We do not grant access based on a user ID supplied by the client. We use rate limits, collect only the data we need, and apply retention limits. Significant policy changes receive a new version date and require your consent again.
